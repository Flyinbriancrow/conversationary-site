Privacy Policy

Conversationary is designed so your conversations stay yours.

Information we collect

Conversationary does not require an account and does not ask you to enter or record your answers. The app does not collect personal information, analytics, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or conversation content.

Information stored on your device

The app stores question progress, skipped-question status, saved bookmarks, and onboarding status locally on your device. This information is used only to operate app features, is not transmitted to the developer, and is removed when you delete the app unless your device or operating-system backup preserves app data.

Purchases

In-app purchases are offered and processed by Apple through the App Store. Conversationary uses Apple’s StoreKit system to check whether a purchase is valid and whether premium content should be unlocked. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details. Apple’s handling of purchase and account information is governed by Apple’s own privacy policies.

Tracking and advertising

Conversationary does not contain third-party advertising, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell or share personal information.

Support communications

If you choose to email support, the developer receives your email address and anything you include in the message. That information is used to respond, troubleshoot the app, and maintain necessary support records. It is not sold or used for advertising. You can avoid sending sensitive information when requesting support.

Children

Conversationary is a general-audience conversation app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before those changes are released.
