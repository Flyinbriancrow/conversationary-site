How do I find a saved question?

Open Conversationary and tap Saved Questions on the home screen. Tap the Save button on any question to add or remove it from that collection.

What happens when I skip a question?

“Skip for now” sets that question aside while the app offers other new questions. It can return after the other unseen questions have been offered. It is not permanently deleted.

Can I revisit completed questions?

Yes. Open a completed topic or pack and choose the browse option. Revisiting a question does not reset or change your progress.

How do I restore question packs?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that originally purchased the packs. A one-time purchase does not expire, but Restore Purchases lets a new or reinstalled device confirm that purchase with Apple.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings and choose Reset question progress. This starts the question rotation over while keeping your saved bookmarks and purchase entitlement.

Does the app save our answers?

No. Conversationary does not record what you say and does not provide a field for entering answers.
